كَلِمَة

Look up Arabic words in English and Urdu, save them, and practice them.

Settings
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save. It stays on this device only.

Enter any noun, verb or adjective, with or without vowel marks. Verbs can be in any tense or person. To find the Arabic for an English or Urdu word, switch to "English or Urdu → Arabic".

Saved words live only on this device. Export a backup now and then, and import it to restore or move to another device.

Answers are generated by Google Gemini. Double-check important vowel marks, conjugations and translations with a dictionary or your teacher.
